3. Privacy Policy

RRIG respects your privacy and follows Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other privacy laws that apply. PrePro is designed so that your music and recordings stay on your device.

3.1 What stays on your device

  • Songs, recordings and versions. Stored in your browser's storage on your device. Not uploaded to RRIG.
  • Microphone audio. Used only while you record, or while Capture is listening. Capture keeps the last 60 seconds in your device's memory only, and it is gone when you close PrePro. It is never uploaded.
  • MIDI input. Read on your device from connected instruments. Not uploaded.
  • Settings and your AI key. Stored on your device. Your AI key is sent only to the AI provider you chose.

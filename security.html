Security and Privacy at PrePro

Version 1.0. Last updated November 7, 2026.
PrePro is owned and operated by Red Road Innovation Group Inc. ("RRIG"). This page explains in plain language how PrePro handles your songs and information today. It is for information only. The Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are the binding documents, and they win if anything here differs.

The short version

  • PrePro has no account system and no song server. Your songs, takes and settings stay on your own device.
  • RRIG cannot see, open or listen to your songs, because they are never sent to us.
  • The AI features use your own AI account. Your key goes from your device straight to the AI company you chose.
  • Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy. PrePro never sees your card number.
  • PrePro does not use advertising, tracking or analytics tools.
  • PrePro does not hold SOC 2, ISO 27001 or any other security certification. See Certifications.

Where your data lives

PrePro runs inside your web browser, or as an installed app that uses the same browser engine. Everything you make is saved in your browser's private storage on that one device:

WhatWhere it is keptWho can reach it
Songs, recordings, MIDI and mixesBrowser storage (IndexedDB) on your deviceYou, on that device and browser
Song versionsBrowser storage (IndexedDB) on your deviceYou, on that device and browser
Settings and preferencesBrowser storage (localStorage) on your deviceYou, on that device and browser
Your AI key, only if you tick "Remember on this device"Browser storage (localStorage) on your deviceYou, on that device and browser
Your PrePro licence key, name and email from your receiptBrowser storage (localStorage) on your deviceYou, and Lemon Squeezy when the licence is checked

Other websites cannot read this storage. Anyone who can use your unlocked device and browser can open PrePro and see your songs, so treat your device like your studio door.

Because your songs are not copied to a server, RRIG cannot recover them if your device is lost, reset or wiped, or if you clear your browser data. Please keep your own backups. Click File, then Save Song File (.prepro), to save a copy you can keep somewhere safe, such as a USB drive or cloud folder.

Your audio recordings are never uploaded by PrePro. The microphone is only used while you are recording or listening, and PrePro closes it when you stop. MIDI devices such as drum kits are only used after you allow them.

Your AI key

  • You create the key in your own account with the AI company, and you pay that company directly.
  • PrePro sends the key only to the AI company you picked. RRIG never receives it.
  • If you do not tick "Remember on this device", the key is forgotten when you close PrePro.
  • To remove a saved key, open the AI window and click Disconnect.
  • Set a monthly spending limit in your AI account. If you think a key was exposed, delete it in your AI account and make a new one.

Payments

Lemon Squeezy is the seller of record for paid plans. Card details are typed into Lemon Squeezy's own checkout page, and Lemon Squeezy says it is responsible for card data security under PCI-DSS. PrePro and RRIG never see or store your card number.

How to protect your work

  1. Lock your phone, tablet or computer with a passcode.
  2. Back up important songs often. Click File, then Save Song File (.prepro).
  3. Do not clear your browser's website data for PrePro unless you have a backup.
  4. On a shared computer, do not tick "Remember on this device" for your AI key.
  5. Keep your browser and operating system up to date.

Certifications and audits

PrePro does not currently hold SOC 2, ISO 27001 or any other security certification, and RRIG has not had an independent security audit. We do not claim to be compliant with any security standard.

The companies PrePro relies on run their own security programs. For example, GitHub publishes SOC 2 Type 2 reports for its services. Those reports cover GitHub, not RRIG or PrePro.

Because PrePro keeps your songs on your device and has no customer database, there is very little customer data in our hands to protect. If PrePro adds cloud storage, sharing or business accounts in the future, we plan to review whether an independent audit such as SOC 2 is right, and we will update this page.

Report a security problem

If you find a security problem in PrePro, please tell us privately before sharing it publicly, so we can fix it. A security contact address will be posted here before paid plans open. Please do not test against other people's devices or accounts.

Changes to this page
